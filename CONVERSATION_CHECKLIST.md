# Conversation readiness checklist

This is an operational check, not a performance benchmark or an approval certificate.

## Availability first

The reviewed public site says release is in preparation. Confirm actual availability and installed-build support before planning a trip around the app.

## Mode and access

Check subscription status. Online modes require internet and credits; Private Cloud Compute has additional compatible iOS/Apple Intelligence and quota requirements. On Device needs supported prepared models and speech recognition.

## Language-pair test

Test question and reply in both directions, including a number, a date and a place name. Read the original transcript before judging the translation. Website language choices do not establish speech support.

## At the table

Keep the microphone unobstructed, reduce competing speech and agree to take turns. Pause on a bad transcript rather than building a longer conversation on it.

## Pass criteria

Fresh speech from both people is recognized and translated in the selected mode. Important details are independently confirmed. Offline readiness is tested without network access, not inferred from readable saved history.

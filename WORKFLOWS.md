# LingoVela: Live Translate — practical workflows

These are suggested user procedures, not claims that automated tests have been performed. Each workflow has a concrete outcome to inspect.

## A travel question and its reply

Choose the two languages before approaching the other speaker. Begin with a short question such as which platform a train leaves from. Wait for the spoken translation, then invite the reply. Read back the platform number, time and direction individually. Do not treat a confident voice as evidence that a proper name was recognized correctly.

## An iPad conversation across a table

Place the iPad where both people can see the text and the microphone is unobstructed. Reduce competing audio and agree to take turns. Use the visible speaker labels to follow which language belongs to each person. This is a shared-device conversation; it should not be assumed to provide separate remote participant channels.

## Prepare for limited connectivity

While online, activate access, download the needed models and verify the language pair. Then test On Device with connectivity unavailable using fresh speech from both speakers. Ensure spoken input is recognized, not merely that previously translated text can be displayed. Keep an alternative communication method if either speech recognition or the language pair is unavailable.

## Language practice with a friend

Start with an everyday scenario and three short turns per person. Compare each original transcript to the intended sentence before judging the translation. Ask the other speaker to explain an ambiguous expression. Save only the conversation material you intend to retain; do not assume a translator is grading your pronunciation or proficiency.

## A conversation with important details

Prepare names and key vocabulary in text before beginning. Repeat dates, amounts and instructions slowly and verify them against a written source. For medical or legal matters, use an appropriate qualified interpreter when accuracy is consequential. The app can assist a conversation but does not certify the meaning of an instruction.
